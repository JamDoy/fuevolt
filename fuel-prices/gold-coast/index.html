Fuel Prices in Gold Coast

The Gold Coast is covered by Queensland's government fuel price transparency scheme, the same official data source FueVolt uses across South East Queensland. As a major tourist destination, prices at beachfront and tourist-strip stations can differ noticeably from suburban ones further inland.

Price Trends in Gold Coast

Gold Coast prices generally follow the same cycle pattern seen across South East Queensland. Tourist-strip stations near major attractions and the beachfront tend to sit above suburban prices further inland, which is typical of tourist-destination fuel markets generally.

Tips to Save on Fuel in Gold Coast

If you're visiting the Gold Coast, comparing prices on FueVolt before you arrive can help you avoid overpaying at tourist-strip stations. For locals, suburban stations further from the beachfront and major attractions are generally more competitively priced.
