Price Trends in Sunshine Coast

Sunshine Coast prices generally follow the same cycle pattern seen across South East Queensland. Tourist-strip stations in beachfront precincts like Noosa and Mooloolaba tend to charge more than stations further inland — typical of coastal tourist destinations generally.

Tips to Save on Fuel in Sunshine Coast

Compare prices before heading to the beach — beachside stations in tourist precincts tend to charge more than suburban alternatives just inland. Checking FueVolt before you set off can help you avoid the premium.
