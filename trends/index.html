How Trends Are Built

FueVolt doesn't keep a central price-history database — each station's trend is built from real prices recorded on your own device the times you've checked that station before. A station you're viewing for the first time won't have a graph yet; check back on it over time to see its trend build up.

This keeps trend data accurate to what was actually reported, rather than estimated or backfilled. See our Privacy Policy for details on what's stored locally versus sent anywhere else.

Understanding the Fuel Price Cycle

Fuel prices in Australia's capital cities don't just drift up and down randomly — they follow a well-documented petrol price cycle, tracked by the ACCC in Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Adelaide and Perth. Prices typically rise sharply over a day or two, then decline gradually over the next one to two weeks before spiking again.

Knowing where a city sits in its cycle is the best way to time a fill-up. Read our full guide on how fuel price cycles work in Australia and the best day to buy fuel.
