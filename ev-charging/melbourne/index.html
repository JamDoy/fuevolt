EV Charging Stations in Melbourne

Melbourne's EV charging infrastructure spans the CBD, inner suburbs, and an expanding network of fast chargers along major arterials and regional routes like the Great Ocean Road and the Hume Freeway.

Live Snapshot: EV Charging in Melbourne

As of 22 August 2026, FueVolt tracks 45 charging locations within 15km of central Melbourne, including 30 fast or ultra-rapid (50kW+) chargers — 16 with Type 2, 33 with CCS2, 19 with CHAdeMO. This snapshot refreshes daily from Open Charge Map, the same data source used by FueVolt's live charger search.

Charging Coverage in Melbourne

FueVolt lists chargers across inner Melbourne, the eastern and south-eastern suburbs, the western suburbs including Werribee and Sunshine, and outer growth corridors like Cranbourne and Craigieburn. Shopping centres, council car parks, and highway service centres each contribute to the network, with regional Victoria increasingly connected via fast-charging corridors.

Charging Tips for Melbourne

For trips out of Melbourne, plan charging stops along known fast-charging corridors before you set off, since regional coverage is thinner than in the metro area. Within the city, workplace and retail chargers are convenient for topping up during the day, while ultra-rapid chargers near major shopping strips suit a quick top-up between errands.
